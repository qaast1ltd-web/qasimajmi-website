Professional Profile

Dedicated to Excellence in Spinal Care

Mr. Qasim Silman Ajmi is a fellowship-trained orthopaedic spinal surgeon with a specialist interest in minimally invasive spinal surgery (MIS). His clinical training integrates dual orthopaedic and neurosurgical spinal principles.

Having completed high-volume international and national spinal surgery fellowships—including Boston Medical Center (USA), Klinikum rechts der Isar (TU Munich, Germany), Royal Preston Hospital, and Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust (London)—Mr. Ajmi possesses broad expertise in managing complex cervical, thoracic, and lumbar pathologies.

He actively incorporates evidence-based medical techniques, innovative decision-support tools, and multidisciplinary teamwork to deliver optimal outcomes for patients suffering from spinal degeneration, spinal trauma, deformity, and nerve compression.

Clinical Specialisations

Surgical Expertise & Spine Procedures

Combining minimally invasive techniques with robust surgical stabilization to reduce recovery times and optimize spinal health.

Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery (MIS)

Advanced tubular decompressions, MIS-ATP (Anterior-to-Psoas), OLIF, TLIF, and percutaneous pedicle screw fixations designed for minimal tissue disruption and faster rehabilitation.

  • MIS Antepsoas (ATP) Fusion
  • Percutaneous Screws
  • Day-case Lumbar Discectomy

Cervical Spine Surgery

Comprehensive management for cervical spondylotic myelopathy, disc herniation, and spinal instability with precise anterior and posterior decompression techniques.

  • ACDF (Anterior Fusion)
  • Cervical Corpectomy
  • Posterior Fixation & Laminectomy

Targeted Spinal Injections

Diagnostic and therapeutic spinal pain management under fluoroscopic guidance to relieve localized inflammation and nerve compression without invasive surgery.

  • Caudal & Cervical Epidurals
  • Facet Joint Injections
  • Selective Transforaminal Blocks

Kyphoplasty & Vertebroplasty

Minimally invasive cement augmentation for acute painful osteoporotic wedge compression fractures and pathological spinal lesions to restore height and stability.

  • Unipedicular Kyphoplasty
  • Vertebral Height Restoration
  • Rapid Fracture Pain Relief

Spinal Trauma & Deformity

Emergency and complex reconstructive surgery for spinal fractures, adult degenerative scoliosis, spondylolisthesis, and spinal tumor decompression across Major Trauma Centres.

  • Major Trauma Reconstruction
  • Degenerative Spondylolisthesis
  • Tumor Decompression

Cauda Equina Emergency Evaluation

Rapid diagnostic protocols and urgent surgical decompression for acute neurological compromise, lumbar disc herniations, and cauda equina syndrome.

  • Urgent Neurological Triage
